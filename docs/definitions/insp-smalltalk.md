# Smalltalk

The purest form of the residential ideal. The system is an *image*: a persistent,
running world of objects that you inhabit and modify from inside, in Smalltalk
itself, with no edit-compile-run seam. Smalltalk is written in Smalltalk, so the
tools that browse and change the system are made of the same stuff as the system.

**Lights up:** the residential invariant of [[live-environment]]. The environment
is the program, self-hosted, live and self-describing. This is the feel chirality is
after.

**chirality takes:** self-hosting ([[thesis]]), everything-is-a-uniform-thing (there
objects, here typed processes, P2), and living inside a running world rather than
editing dead files.

**chirality rejects:** the single image. One heap means no isolation, no crash
containment, no capabilities, one bad `doesNotUnderstand:` away from a wedged
world. chirality keeps the residential feel and replaces the image with the mesh
([[node-architecture]]). It also rejects untyped objects: authority and cost live
in the type here, not in convention.
