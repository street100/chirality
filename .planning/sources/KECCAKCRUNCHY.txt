<h1 class="page-title">The <span class='sc'>Keccak</span> Crunchy Crypto Collision and Pre-image Contest</h1>
</div>
</header>
<div class="container">
<p>This page specifies the challenges, defines the rules and reports on the status of the <span class='sc'>Keccak</span> Crunchy Crypto Collision and Pre-image Contest.</p>
<p class="well well-sm"><b>Summary of the results:</b> The best preimage solution was on 5 rounds and was submitted by Xiaoen Lin, Hongbo Yu, Enming Dong, Wenhao Wu and Yantian Shen in October 2025. The best collision was on 6 rounds and was submitted by Ling Song, Guohong Liao and again Jian Guo in February 2017. Remarkably, the smaller versions are harder to break. Although they have a smaller state, they offer much less degrees of freedom, especially relative to the capacity that is the same for all versions.</p>

<h1>Rules</h1>
<p>We present challenges for reduced-round <span class='sc'>Keccak</span> instances, namely <span class='sc'>Keccak</span>[<i>c</i>=160, <i>r</i>=<i>b</i>-<i>c</i>] with <i>b</i> ≥ 200:</p>
<ul>
<li>The capacity is fixed to 160 bits: this implies a security level of 2<sup>80</sup> against generic collision search.</li>
<li>The width <i>b</i> of <span class='sc'>Keccak</span>-<i>f</i>[<i>b</i>] is in {200, 400, 800, 1600}: the width values that support the chosen capacity.</li>
<li>The number of rounds <i>n</i><sub><i>r</i></sub> ranges from 1 to 12.</li>
<li>The submitter is free to choose the subsequence of consecutive rounds of <span class='sc'>Keccak</span>-<i>f</i>[<i>b</i>], e.g., the <i>n</i><sub><i>r</i></sub> first rounds, the <i>n</i><sub><i>r</i></sub> last rounds or <i>n</i><sub><i>r</i></sub> rounds starting anywhere. This impacts the values of the round constants in the reduced-round <span class='sc'>Keccak</span>-<i>f</i>[<i>b</i>].</li>
</ul>
<p>For each of these <span class='sc'>Keccak</span> instances there are two challenges:</p>
<ul>
<li>generating a collision in the output truncated to 160 bits;</li>
<li>generating a pre-image of an output truncated to 80 bits.</li>
</ul>
<p>Solutions shall be sent to <code>all</code> <em>-at-</em> <code>keccak.team</code> with:</p>
<ul>
<li>subject "[CRUNCHY CONTEST] <i>n</i><sub><i>r</i></sub> rounds, width <i>b</i>, challenge type", with <i>n</i><sub><i>r</i></sub> and <i>b</i> having the corresponding values and challenge type either "collision" or "pre-image";</li>
<li>in the body:<ul>
<li>names and affiliations of authors;</li>
<li>round index of the first round of the reduced-round variant of <span class='sc'>Keccak</span>-<i>f</i>[<i>b</i>];</li>
<li>colliding messages or pre-image in hexadecimal notation;</li>
<li>textual summary of how the result was obtained.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<p>The prize money is distributed in the following way:</p>
<ul>
<li>There is one prize per challenge.</li>
<li>For a given challenge, only the <strong>first</strong> received solution gets a prize.</li>
<li>The prize for a challenge for <i>n</i><sub><i>r</i></sub> rounds is <i>n</i><sub><i>r</i></sub>×10 &euro;.</li>
</ul>
<p><span class="sc">KeccakTools</span> contains support for the validation of solutions (see file <a href="https://github.com/KeccakTeam/KeccakTools/blob/master/Sources/KeccakCrunchyContest.cpp"><code>KeccakCrunchyContest.cpp</code></a> ).</p>
